Start with one application. Bring your facts and approved answers; review the employer's form before you submit.


    

  1. Prepare your profile and answers
    Put your résumé in resumes/ and set resumePath in config/profile.json. Replace all example values. In config/answers.json, keep only answers you approve, or use {}. Review the effective values in Answers & profile. After editing local files, click Sync.
    2. 

  2. Add application links or sync Notion
    Click Add links and paste direct employer job/application URLs, one per line. Add to queue saves and selects them without starting work. If you have configured your own Notion pipeline, click Sync; new Notion jobs arrive unselected. Manual links stay local.
    3. 

  3. Select and review your queue
    Use row checkboxes or Select page, then open Review queue to inspect the batch. Clear a checkbox to leave a job tracked without running it. In Configuration, begin with one application at a time and deterministic answer matching. Enable continuation to move between supported application steps.
    4. 

  4. Run autofill
    Click Run queue. Formwright opens its own browser session and fills supported fields from your supplied facts. Tabs stay open for review. Pause queue lets active applications finish before stopping pending work. Login, CAPTCHA, MFA and email verification may need your help in the browser.
    5. 

  5. Resolve missing information and review
    Open Needs attention and the affected row's Details. Where offered, Save answers & queue retry saves answers for that application and selects a retry; use Run queue to start it. It does not change the global Answer Bank. Check every field, résumé upload and step in the employer's tab, even when autofill says Ready for review.
    6. 

  6. Submit yourself, then update tracking
    Complete remaining fields and submit in the employer's browser tab. After the employer confirms submission, use Mark applied or choose Applied in the row's status. This records your submission; it does not submit a form. Notion writes are read back before being shown as saved.
    7. 



Understand the status before retrying




Ready for review
Autofill has finished its supported work. Review the full application; it has not been submitted by the dashboard.


Needs information
Read the row's missing questions and provide explicit answers where offered. Uploads, consent and unsupported controls may need manual completion.


Error or Interrupted
Read Details, inspect the employer's tab and correct the cause before using the offered retry control.


Verify submission
Check the employer's confirmation or application history first. Avoid submitting the same application twice.


Apply / Applied
Tracking intent and outcome: Apply means you plan to apply; Applied means you have submitted. Tracking stays separate from autofill progress.




When something needs your help


No jobs appear
Add a direct application link, populate data/jobs.txt, or configure Notion and click Sync. A new installation starts with an empty job list.


A source refresh failed
Open the relevant source view, correct its token, mapping or file, and click Sync. Notion jobs are rechecked before running; a stale snapshot cannot be used to start them.


An upload, answer or login was rejected
Inspect the employer's tab. Check your résumé path and the accepted file type. Use an explicit approved answer that matches the employer's question. Complete verification and unsupported controls manually, then review the full form.


Optional integrations and private data
Notion, shared login and LM Studio are optional. In Notion, only nonempty Confirmed answers with Auto-fill OK checked are used. Shared credentials require allowed HTTPS hostnames. Keep private config, résumés, browser sessions and output out of GitHub. Keep the dashboard on localhost.
